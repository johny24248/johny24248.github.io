Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to Jumper and other mobile games developed by FatDev, an individual indie developer ("we", "us").

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not personally collect, store, or share any personal data. However, our games use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.

Our third-party advertising partner, Google AdMob, may automatically collect data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and device characteristics to personalize and serve advertisements. The game engine we use (Unity) may also collect limited technical device information needed for the game to run.

2. Third-Party Services

Our games integrate Google AdMob. For details on how Google handles your information, please review the Google Privacy Policy and how AdMob uses data.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

3. Children's Privacy

Our games do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided personal information via ad identifiers, please contact us so that we can take necessary actions.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

5. Contact Us
